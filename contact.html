Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need to create an account?

A: No! CompatiX is completely free and open. No login or account creation required.

Q: How accurate are the compatibility results?

A: Our results are based on official game/app requirements. However, actual performance may vary based on driver versions, system load, and other factors.

Q: Can I add my own game to the database?

A: Not yet, but we're working on community contributions. Contact us with suggestions!

Q: Is my data stored?

A: No. Each compatibility check is stateless. We don't store personal information or browsing history.
