About CompatiX

Know before you run — here's what CompatiX is all about.

OUR PURPOSE

Why CompatiX Exists

It is frustrating to install a game or app only to discover afterward that your PC cannot run it well. CompatiX removes that uncertainty upfront by comparing your hardware with the requirements that matter.

It is built for gamers, students, and anyone buying, upgrading, or simply curious about what their PC or laptop can handle. The promise is simple: fast, free, and completely open. Enter your specs and check without creating an account or signing up.

TRANSPARENCY

Data & Accuracy

Game data is sourced from the RAWG API, while app data is curated manually. When exact requirements are not available for a title, Specter AI estimates them and clearly flags the estimate in the results. CompatiX does not require accounts and does not track or store personal data; everything runs client-side or through stateless requests.
